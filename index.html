Honors research

Local Sentiment and Alpha

Business Honors capstone, advised by Dr. Jinghan Cai, May 2026

305S&P 500 firms
2012–2020daily returns
3 levelscounty, state, national

Does the mood around a company’s headquarters move its stock? I built a panel of about 593,000 firm-day observations and matched geotagged Twitter sentiment to daily abnormal returns (CAPM and Fama–French three-factor) with firm and month fixed effects.

County-level sentiment showed no significant link to returns. State and national sentiment did, though the effects were small. The national link held, though more weakly, after removing sentiment tied to financial conditions.
